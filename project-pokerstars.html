Overview

Designing and adapting flows to comply with international iGaming regulations
Project's context
Flutter entertainment is a company active in the gaming and gambling industry. Part of it are the PokerStars brand and the lately acquired Italian company Sisal.
Sisal and PokerStars platforms
Sisal and PokerStars operate separate gaming platforms for betting, casino games and slots. To simplify business and IT systems, PokerStars has begun migrating to the same systems used by Sisal.
Problem statement and requirements
New legal requirements prompted a review of user flows such as login, account settings and deactivation. They apply to Portugal, Spain and France, each with specific corner cases, increasing complexity. English was used for official communication and project deliverables.

Exploration phase

Analyzing regulatory requirements and benchmarking existing iGaming platforms
Requirement analysis
Requirements for Spanish, Portuguese and French legal restriction were analyzed in-depth. Miro boards were used for three main processes:

Comparing all countries requirements and track down common use cases and differences

Translating higher-level requirements into practical tasks, user processes and UX corner cases

Annotating open points that needed deeper analysis or to be discussed with legal representatives
